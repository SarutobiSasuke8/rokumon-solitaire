# Playtest Checklist

- [ ] Responsive Layout: Verify cards fit cleanly in Portrait (mobile) & Landscape (desktop/tablet).
- [ ] Touch Controls: Dragging cards on mobile touchscreens is smooth, crisp, and snaps cleanly to drop targets.
- [ ] Rules Logic: Tableau moves require alternating colors & descending ranks. Foundations require ascending rank of matching suit starting with Ace.
- [ ] Stock Recycling: Stock pile recycles waste cards upon reaching the end.
- [ ] Auto-Move: Tapping an available card moves it to Foundation if valid, or lowest legal Tableau stack.
- [ ] Undo Stack: Reverts moves, deck draws, and flips accurately without desynced card count.
- [ ] Audio: Sounds trigger cleanly without delay or audio clipping.
- [ ] Victory Cascade: Winning the game triggers card bounce physics cascade animation.
