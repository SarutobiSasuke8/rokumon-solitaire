How to Play

Objective

Move all 52 cards into the 4 Foundation piles (top right), sorted by suit from Ace to King. This mode is Classic Klondike.

Playfield Layout

  • Stock Pile (top left): Draw 1 or 3 cards at a time, depending on your setting. When the stock is empty, select the ↻ arrow to recycle the waste.
  • Waste Pile (top left): Only the rightmost card can be played.
  • 4 Foundations (top right): Build up each suit starting with Ace: A ➔ 2 ➔ 3 ➔ ... ➔ K.
  • 7 Tableau Columns (bottom): Build down in alternating colours. Try moving 10♥ onto J♣. A King can then fill any empty column.

Controls & Shortcuts

  • Drag & Drop: Drag cards or stacks between columns & foundations.
  • Single / Double Tap: Tap any card to auto-move it to Foundation or legal Tableau pile.
  • Space: Draw stock cards
  • Ctrl + Z / Cmd + Z: Undo move
  • Ctrl + Y / Cmd + Y: Redo move
  • H: Show a move or a stock action that leads to a playable card.
  • A: Auto-complete solver sweep
